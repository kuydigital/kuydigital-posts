## CrowdSense — crowdsense.net

- Free web app that estimates how crowded places in Metro Manila are, so people can avoid crowds and full parking.
- Covers malls, parks and holiday spots around Metro Manila.
- Estimates combine foot-traffic forecasts with predictable factors such as paydays (the 15th and 30th) and events like mall sales, conferences and expos.
- People can send reports of how busy a place is.
- Shows a clear note that its numbers are estimates, not proof of real-time conditions.
- Works on phones; live since October 2026.
- Also on TikTok and Instagram as @crowdsense.net.
